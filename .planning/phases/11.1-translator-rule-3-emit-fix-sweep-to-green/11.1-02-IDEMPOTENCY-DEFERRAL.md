## Why deferred (two independent reasons)

1. **Scope (LOCKED).** `11.1-CONTEXT.md` line 18 — a LOCKED decision under D-01's
   "narrow translator-correctness phase" framing — explicitly lists the **idempotency
   proof** under *"Not in scope (hands back to a re-opened Phase 11)."* That LOCKED
   decision takes precedence over the Plan 02 Task 4 / Plan 03 pre-flight framing, which
   over-scoped it into 11.1. This note reconciles that conflict in favor of CONTEXT.

2. **A naive proof is now infeasible in 11.1 and would regress an OOM fix.** After these
   plans were written (2026-05-19), the `mgga_c_tpssloc` (`260520-eem`) and
   `mgga_c_revtpss` (`260520-k1q`) functionals were converted to a **hierarchical-CSE
   regen + manual sub-crate shard** layout (facade + 7 `_pK` shards each) to clear a
   per-functional proc-macro OOM. A vanilla `python3 tools/maple_to_kernels.py translate
   --family all` (hier-CSE default OFF) emits those two functionals **flat**, so a
   whole-tree zero-diff check *necessarily* shows a large diff for them — and committing
   that regen would clobber the OOM fix. A correct idempotency proof must replay the split
   pipeline (`LIBXC_RS_HIERARCHICAL_CSE=1` regen + `tools/split_per_functional_subcrate.py`)
   for those functionals. That belongs to the re-opened Phase 11.
