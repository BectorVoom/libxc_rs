## Accomplishments

- **Two-stage helper turbofish cooperation** (fix1): restored Block A/B `::<f64>` in family translators; added `_FN_F64_TURBOFISH_RE` retarget pass in `_wrap_f64_literals_v2` to swap `name::<f64>(` → `name::<F>(` for D-09 allowlist members inside chunked bodies.
- **cse pass 2.5 input/output reconciliation** (fix2): bool rematerialization in pass 2 creates new refs to non-bool `tN` vars; pass-1's `last_use` was computed before that and missed them. Pass 2.5 walks every chunk's inputs and ensures the defining chunk exports them. Caught the `t213`/`t218` not-in-scope bug class (1066+ E0425s in gga_c_gaploc).
- **`f64::IDENT` → `F::IDENT` retarget** (fix3): chunked bodies use CubeCL Float-trait associated methods (`F::sqrt`, `F::ln`, `F::exp`, `F::abs`, `F::powf`) and associated constants (`F::EPSILON`, `F::MAX`) instead of concrete `f64::*`. `_F64_QUALIFIED_RE` regex covers it.
- **Integer-mantissa exponent literal wrap** (fix4): `_F64_LITERAL_RE` extended with `|\d+[eE][+-]?\d+` alternative so `1e-21` (no decimal point) is wrapped — was triggering E0277 in `lda_c_pk09/fxc_pol/part2/chunk517.rs`.
- **`emit.clean_subcrate_src` cleanup** (fix5): wipes `<func>/src/` before each per-functional regen so stale `<output>/partN/mod.rs` directories don't co-exist with `<output>/partN.rs` files when the emit-form shifts across regens. Triggered E0761 in `gga_c_ft97` after iter5.
- **`param_*` threading for CSE-chunked emit** (fix6): pre-scan compute_lines for `param_*` identifiers; extend `ambient_pool`; declare in `_build_chunk_wrapper` signature as `f64`. Closes the D-02 ABI RISK note about `param_*` not being threaded. Triggered E0425 in `mgga_c_tpss/lxc_pol/part22/chunk2.rs` after iter6.
- **Full-tree regen iter7**: lda 43/0/0 + gga 131/0/0 + mgga 92/0/0. Committed as `4fc45a5e16` (~32K files net delete-dominated due to stale chunk dirs wiped by fix5).
- **G1 sample sweep iter3 VERDICT: ALL_OK**: 50/50 PASS in pass=1 (no pass-2 fallback). Wall 6491s (~108 min) at `jobs=10`. Peak RSS 28GB on `mgga_c_kcis` (893s). Both Phase-11 blockers GREEN. G3 canary `mgga_c_b94` GREEN.

## Decisions Made

- **Sample roster (50) vs full sweep (266)**: 50-subcrate sample selected to fit a single session's working window. The roster spans every known stress class (Brent, complex 4th-order, Lambert W, param-heavy) plus both Phase-11 blockers. Full sweep deferred. Sample sufficient to claim G1 ALL_OK on a representative substrate; remaining 216 subcrates are likely-but-not-proven.
- **Smoke-gate-before-sweep discipline**: For each translator fix, spot-check 1-2 known-failing exemplars via single `cargo build -p ...` BEFORE running the (multi-hour) sweep. Catches translator gaps in 10 min instead of hours.
- **Two-stage turbofish (not single-stage)**: original Plan 01 design assumed native emit could replace post-emit substitution entirely. Smoke gate disproved that — flat path needs `::<f64>` (concrete wrapper), chunked path needs `::<F>` (generic chunk). The two stages cooperate.
