## Lessons Learned

- **The umbrella crate makes every verify test a 281-kernel build.** Single-functional checks must
  bypass `libxc_rs` (direct kernel call) — the established escape hatch.
- **A "compile-green canary" ≠ a "numerically-correct canary."** The old G3 passed by skipping b94
  entirely. Real parity surfaced a systemic regularization gap.
- **Translating the maple2c expression is necessary but not sufficient** — libxc's `work_*.c` driver
  preprocessing (τ_W clamp, thresholds) is part of the contract and is not yet translated.

## Open Risks / Carry-Forward

- `LIBXC_RS_BYPASS_DEFERRED` env-gate still in use; removal is 11-08 Task 2 (Phase 11).
