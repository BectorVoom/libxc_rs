## Caveats / known overrides

- The 50K → 500K bump in `split_oversized_kernel.py` and `split_oversized_mgga.py`
  contradicts an in-source comment that pinned the budget per CONTEXT D-09
  ("sub-crate re-bin-packing is OUT of scope for Phase 9"). Override accepted by
  user.
- The bump means future translation/splitting passes will produce *fewer, larger*
  sub-crates. This reverses the rustc-OOM mitigation strategy documented in
  `docs/manual/Cubecl/cubecl_macro_fanout_manual.md` §16. If a Phase 10+
  re-translation runs into rustc OOM, expect to revisit this constant.
- 3 splitters previously hard-coded `WORKSPACE = "/workspace"` (a container-era
  absolute path). They now derive `REPO_ROOT` from `__file__`, matching the
  pattern used by the other splitters. This makes them runnable on any host.

## What did NOT happen

- No re-translation. `batch_translate_mgga.py` was *not* invoked — the constant
  is bumped but it would re-translate ~85 MGGA functionals from Maple oracle
  data; that is a major rebuild, far outside a quick task. If the user wants
  the new bin budget actually applied to MGGA, run `batch_translate_mgga.py`
  in a follow-up.
- No `kernel-lda` un-split + re-split. To take advantage of the 10× budget on
  LDA you would have to revert the existing split (move `kernel-lda-1..4`
  contents back into `kernel-lda/src/` and delete the sub-crates) before
  re-running `split_lda_subcrates.py`. That was not requested and is also a
  major rebuild.
