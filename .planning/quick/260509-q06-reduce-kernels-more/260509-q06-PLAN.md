# Quick Task 260509-q06 — Reduce kernels more (mild package)

## Why

After q04 (LDA 4→2) and q05 (GGA 59→8, MGGA 109→14), the user wants
further reduction.

Choice: mild package (preserves OOM headroom):

| Family | Current | Target | Limit |
|--------|--------:|-------:|------:|
| LDA   | 1+2 = 3 | 1+1 = 2 | `--target-max=200000` |
| GGA   | 1+8 = 9 | 1+5 = 6 | `--bin-limit=500000` |
| MGGA  | 1+14 = 15 | 1+8 = 9 | `--target-max=600000` |
| **Sum** | **27** | **17** | -10 sub-crates |

GGA at 500K and MGGA at 600K both produce 0 solo-oversized bins (the
largest single functionals — gga_c_lyp ~277K, mgga_c_tpssloc 556K —
fit individually under their respective caps). LDA at 200K is enough
for a single bin (total 187K).

## Pre-requisite fix (already shipped this task: commit `beddd1b0`)

`tools/resplit_gga.py:create_subcrate()` used `shutil.copytree`, leaving
each per-functional dir behind in `kernel-gga/src/` after q05's repack.
1810 orphan files / 137 MB on disk that were byte-identical duplicates
of the live `kernel-gga-1..8/src/` trees. Fixed by switching to
`shutil.move` (matches the docstring) and removing the orphan trees
under `git rm -r`.

This had to land before q06 ran the GGA repack, otherwise the merge
guard in `gather_into_aggregator()` would (correctly) abort.

## Tasks

1. **LDA**: `python3 tools/split_lda_subcrates.py --repack --target-max=200000`
   - Expect: 1+1 = 2 sub-crates. -1 from current. lda_c_pk09 etc. all
     fit in one bin since total is 187K << 200K.
2. **GGA**: `python3 tools/resplit_gga.py --bin-limit 500000`
   - Expect: 1+5 = 6 sub-crates. -3 from current.
3. **MGGA**: `python3 tools/rebatch_mgga.py --target-max 600000`
   - Expect: 1+8 = 9 sub-crates. -6 from current.

Each family gets its own structural commit. Tools already extended in
q05 (and q06's pre-req fix) — no further tool changes needed.

## must_haves

- truths:
  - LDA sub-crate count: 3 → 2.
  - GGA sub-crate count: 9 → 6.
  - MGGA sub-crate count: 15 → 9.
  - Aggregators (kernel-lda, kernel-gga, kernel-mgga) re-export every
    functional unchanged.

  - No oversized-solo bins.
- artifacts:
  - 4 commits: orphan fix (already landed), LDA, GGA, MGGA, docs.

## Out of scope

- `cargo check` per sub-crate. Defer to user (RAM-constrained per
  memory). The mild package keeps all bins under 600K so risk is low.
- `src/eval/{gga,mgga}_dispatch/` regeneration — q05 follow-up that
  q06 inherits. q06 makes the dispatch staleness worse (numeric
  IDs change again).

## Risks accepted

- Repacking changes numeric IDs again (kernel-gga-1..5 instead of 1..8;
  kernel-mgga-1..8 instead of 1..14). `src/eval/{gga,mgga}_dispatch/`
  references will get further behind. Already a known follow-up;
  staleness compounds but the fix path (regenerate dispatch) is
  unchanged.
- 1M+ line MGGA bins are not exercised by q06 (mild keeps 600K cap).
  Users wanting maximum reduction can re-run with higher limits later.
